## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what personal data ComplianceWatch ("the service", operated by
[Company legal name]) collects when a business or its adviser uses the web app, the WhatsApp
reminders or the partner API, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and what you can ask
us to do with it. It is written to meet the notice requirement of the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023 ("the Act") and is provided in English and Hindi [Hindi text to follow].

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis under the Act |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, mobile number of the account holder | To create the account, sign you in and contact you about the service | Consent at sign-up |
| WhatsApp number | To send reminders you asked for, and to answer your messages | Consent by opting in (see whatsapp-consent.md) |
| Business identifiers: PAN, GSTIN, legal and trade name, state of registration, business category, turnover band, filing scheme and the other profile attributes | To work out which GST obligations apply to the business and when they are due | Consent at onboarding; the profile is the purpose of the service |
| Evidence files you upload against an obligation | To keep a record that an obligation was met | Consent at upload |
| Questions you ask and the answers given | To answer, to improve answers, and for the review of answers by our analysts | Consent at onboarding; questions are logged |
| Technical logs: IP address, device, timestamps | Security, fraud prevention, keeping the service running | Legitimate use for security [lawyer to confirm the Act's term] |

A PAN or GSTIN identifies a business, and in the case of a proprietorship also identifies a
person. We treat them as personal data throughout.

## 3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not send marketing on
WhatsApp; reminders are only what you opted in to. Model providers we use to read regulations
and draft answers receive your questions with identifiers masked and do not retain them
[confirm per provider contract].

## 4. Who we share it with

- Service providers that host and run the service in India (cloud hosting in the Mumbai region
  is the design assumption), send messages (Meta for WhatsApp, an email provider) and process
  payments. Each is bound by contract to use the data only for the service.
- Your chartered accountant or adviser, when you link your business to their account.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

See [data-map.md](data-map.md) for each field. In short: the account and profile for as long
as the account exists and 30 days after a deletion request; evidence files 90 days after a
deletion request for the audit trail [lawyer to confirm]; technical logs 90 days; the anonymous
eval sets our analysts build from questions, indefinitely, with identifiers removed.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Act you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used, ask
for corrections, ask for erasure, nominate a person to exercise these rights for you, and
withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops the processing that relied on it (for
WhatsApp reminders, reply STOP; for the account, use the settings page or write to the
grievance officer). We answer within [30] days.

## 7. Complaints

Write to the grievance officer above. If you are not satisfied with the answer you may
approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 8. Changes to this notice

Each change is a new version. The version you agreed to is recorded with your consent, and
we ask again when the purposes change.
