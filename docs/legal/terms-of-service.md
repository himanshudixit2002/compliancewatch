## 1. The service

ComplianceWatch reads public regulatory documents, keeps a rulebook of GST obligations, and
tells a business which obligations apply to it, when they are due and what to do, on the web
and on WhatsApp. It also answers questions with citations to the underlying documents.

## 2. What it is not

The service is not legal, tax or accounting advice and does not replace a chartered
accountant. Rules are extracted from public documents by software and reviewed by analysts;
they can be late, incomplete or wrong. Every obligation and answer cites its source so you can
check it. You remain responsible for your filings and payments.
